# Assay evidence-quality rubric

Apply this rubric to each extracted assay record. It supplements, but does not
replace, the evidence tiers in `protocol.md`.

| Domain | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chemical identity | unresolved/name only | CID or structure, stereochemistry incomplete | exact CID/structure and tested form resolved |
| Assay context | missing | system or endpoint partly specified | target/system, endpoint, units, and conditions specified |
| Controls | absent/not reported | limited or indirect | assay-appropriate positive, negative, and interference controls |
| Concentration/dose | absent | single concentration or unclear exposure | concentration-response or interpretable dose range |
| Replication/uncertainty | absent | replicate count or variability partly reported | independent/technical replication and uncertainty reported |
| Orthogonal confirmation | absent | one assay format | independent assay, genetic, pharmacological, or orthogonal confirmation |

Do not sum this rubric into a universal biological activity score. Use it to
describe evidence completeness and to prioritize validation.
