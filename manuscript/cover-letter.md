# Draft cover letter

**To the Editor-in-Chief, *Phytochemistry Reviews***

Dear Editor,

Please consider our review, “What Does ‘Active’ Mean for a Terpene? An
Identity-Resolved Evidence Map of Terpene Bioassay Activity,” for publication
in *Phytochemistry Reviews*.

Terpene bioactivity is widely discussed across natural-products chemistry,
pharmacology, microbiology, and toxicology, but the literature often combines
chemical mixtures, database annotations, computational predictions, and
compound-level assay results. Our review addresses this interpretive problem
with a reproducible evidence-map workflow. It links exact chemical identity and
stereochemistry to assay-level records, preserves endpoint and unit semantics,
and grades claims according to target context, controls, replication, and
translation boundaries.

The Terpedia GCP evidence layer provides a versioned denominator for assay
coverage, while underlying PubChem BioAssay records are independently checked
before being promoted to measured activity. The review’s central contribution
is methodological and substantive: it identifies which terpene activity claims
are directly supported, which are context-limited, and which remain falsifiable
hypotheses. It does not create a universal activity score or infer clinical
efficacy from in-vitro signals.

This topic fits the journal’s interest in reviews that advance understanding of
phytochemistry and the effects of specialized plant compounds on other
organisms. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere,
and all authors will approve the submitted version.

Thank you for your consideration.
