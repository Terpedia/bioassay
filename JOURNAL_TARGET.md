# Journal target and A+ standard

## Selected journal

**Phytochemistry Reviews** (Springer Nature; ISSN 1572-980X), the review and
perspective journal of the Phytochemical Society of Europe.

The target is a strong fit because the journal publishes reviews that advance
plant knowledge and covers phytochemistry, plant function and biosynthesis,
and the effects of specialized plant compounds on other organisms. This
manuscript contributes a defined, cross-disciplinary review of terpene
bioassay evidence with a reproducible identity and assay-quality framework.

Journal fit was checked against the publisher/journal scope on 2026-09-04:
<https://link.springer.com/journal/11101>.

## Working title

**What Does “Active” Mean for a Terpene? An Identity-Resolved Evidence Map of
Terpene Bioassay Activity**

## A+ manuscript standard

The manuscript is ready for submission only when it has:

1. a frozen, reproducible search strategy and screening log;
2. a PRISMA-ScR-style flow diagram and explicit inclusion/exclusion rules;
3. identity-safe compound records preserving stereochemistry and formulation;
4. assay-level extraction of target, system, endpoint, controls, concentration,
   replication, and uncertainty;
5. a quantitative evidence map that never conflates assay coverage with
   positive activity;
6. independent quality checks on a sample of extracted studies;
7. figures and tables that make evidence tiers and uncertainty inspectable;
8. a discussion that distinguishes biochemical signal, cellular phenotype,
   animal outcome, exposure, and clinical relevance;
9. complete DOI/PMID provenance and a versioned data/code release; and
10. a concise cover letter explaining the methodological contribution and why
    the synthesis is relevant to phytochemistry readers.

## Central contribution

The paper will not attempt to rank terpenes by a universal “activity score.” It
will show which activity claims survive identity, assay-context, control, and
translation checks, and which remain hypotheses requiring validation.

## Current gap to close

The Terpedia GCP snapshot currently provides PubChem CID-to-assay coverage
metadata, not assay-result records. The next milestone is to retrieve and
extract the underlying assay-level records for prioritized terpene CIDs, while
retaining the GCP snapshot as the coverage denominator.
